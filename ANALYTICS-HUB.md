# Nfinite Creators 0.58.0: PairOfDice Analytics Hub

Adds an admin-only Analytics Hub and begins first-party site-wide page/engagement tracking. Existing qualified audio, app surfaces, creator analytics, commerce attribution, streaming earnings, and payout data remain unchanged.

The hub reports audience, pageviews, engaged time, traffic sources, top pages, qualified streams, listening time, listeners, completion, skip rate, listening surfaces, top tracks, creator growth, creator purchases, and creator-attributed revenue.

GA4 remains complementary for deeper acquisition/campaign analysis. Site-wide page history begins with 0.58.0 and is not retroactively backfilled.
